# Getting Help

Thanks for using Oeros! Here's where to ask so that the right people see your question.

## Questions about Oeros

Go to **[Oeros Discussions](https://github.com/oerosproject/community/discussions)** for anything specific to Oeros:

- The RFCs, specifications and ADRs in [community](https://github.com/oerosproject/community)
- The Oeros ROS 2 container images and [oeros-container-tests](https://github.com/oerosproject/oeros-container-tests)
- Building and configuring [meta-oeros](https://github.com/oerosproject/meta-oeros) and [meta-bootc](https://github.com/oerosproject/meta-bootc)
- The roadmap, and ideas for what to try next

## Questions about ROS on OpenEmbedded and Yocto

For meta-ros recipes, or ROS on Yocto in general, go to the wider ROS OpenEmbedded community:

- **[meta-ros Discussions](https://github.com/ros/meta-ros/discussions)**, including [Q&A](https://github.com/ros/meta-ros/discussions/categories/q-a)
- **[ROS Discourse, OpenEmbedded category](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/c/community-groups/openembedded/26)**
- **[OpenEmbedded channel on the Open Robotics Zulip](https://openrobotics.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/526059-OpenEmbedded)** for real-time chat

## Community Working Group meetings

You're welcome to join the biweekly **ROS OpenEmbedded Community Working Group** meeting. It's open to everyone, from long-time maintainers to people who are just curious. It's a good place to meet the people behind meta-ros and Oeros, ask questions and pick something to work on.

- How to join: [ROS OpenEmbedded Community Group Details](https://github.com/ros/meta-ros/discussions/1632)
- Past meetings: [Meeting Minutes](https://github.com/ros/meta-ros/discussions/categories/meeting-minutes) in the meta-ros Discussions

## Bugs, feature requests and security issues

- **Bugs you can reproduce, and feature requests:** open an issue in the repository they affect.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** **don't** post these publicly. Follow our [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Before you ask

It helps to include:

- The Yocto release and ROS distro, for example Wrynose and Lyrical
- Your `MACHINE`, or the container image tag you're using
- What you ran, what you expected, and what actually happened, with the relevant log excerpt

Please follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in every community space.
